🕰️ How CAT Has Changed Over 25 Years
The exam has undergone 5 major format changes
2000–2008 • Paper-Based Era
Pen & Paper MCQs
150-185 questions • 120-150 min • No sectional time limit • All MCQ
2009–2013 • Early Computer Era
Shifted to CBT (Prometric)
60-90 questions • 135-170 min • 2 sections • All MCQ • Major technical glitches
2014–2019 • Modern Era
TITA Questions Introduced
100 questions • 180 min • 3 sections • MCQ + TITA (non-MCQ, no negative marking)
2020–2021 • COVID Era
Reduced Format
76 questions • 120 min • 3 slots introduced • Multiple sessions for safety
2022–2025 • Current Format
Further Streamlined
66 questions • 120 min • 40 min/section • 3 slots • MCQ + TITA

🎯 Strategy Guide

Data-backed strategies from 25 years of analysis — specifically for you, Aditi!

💪 Aditi, remember: CAT rewards smart work over hard work

You don't need to attempt all 66 questions. You need to pick the RIGHT 45-50 and nail them with 85%+ accuracy.

⏱️ Time Management — The 40-40-40 Rule
Each section = 40 minutes. No going back. Here's your strategy:

📖 VARC (40 min)

Target: 16-18 correct out of 24

• RC (28-30 min): Do 4-5 passages × 6 min

• VA (10-12 min): PJ, PS, Odd One Out

• Skip 2-3 toughest RC questions

• Read the passage ONCE properly — don't re-read

• Para Jumbles: Start from options, work backward

📊 DILR (40 min)

Target: 12-15 correct out of 20

• First 5 min: Scan ALL sets, rank solvability

• Pick 3-4 easiest sets: Solve completely

• 10 min max per set — move on if stuck

• Skip 1-2 hardest sets entirely

• TITA in DILR? Always attempt!

🔢 QA (40 min)

Target: 15-17 correct out of 22

• Pass 1 (15 min): All easy questions first

• Pass 2 (15 min): Medium questions

• Pass 3 (10 min): Hard ones you know

• Skip P&C/Geometry if unsure (-1 hurts!)

• TITA in QA? Always guess intelligently
