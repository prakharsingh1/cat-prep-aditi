Circular Track — Same & Opposite DirectionCAT 2021 Slot 3Hard
Mira and Amal walk along a circular track, starting from the same point at the same time. If they walk in the same direction, then in 45 minutes, Amal completes exactly 3 more rounds than Mira. If they walk in opposite directions, then they meet for the first time exactly after 3 minutes. The number of rounds Mira walks in one hour is: [TITA]
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 8
Let time per round for Amal = a min, Mira = m min.
Same direction: 45/a − 45/m = 3 → 1/a − 1/m = 1/15 ...(1)
Opposite direction: 3/a + 3/m = 1 → 1/a + 1/m = 1/3 ...(2)
From (2)−(1): 2/m = 1/3 − 1/15 = 4/15 → m = 7.5 min
Rounds in 60 min = 60/7.5 = 8
Circular Track — Meeting at Starting Point (LCM)CAT 2020 Slot 3Medium
Anil, Sunil, and Ravi run along a circular path of length 3 km, starting from the same point at the same time, and in the same direction. If their speeds are 15 km/h, 10 km/h, and 8 km/h, respectively, how much distance (in km) will Ravi have run when Anil and Sunil meet again for the first time at the starting point? [TITA]
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 4.8 km
Anil's lap time = 3/15 = 1/5 hr = 12 min. Sunil's lap time = 3/10 hr = 18 min.
Meet at start = LCM(12, 18) = 36 min = 3/5 hr.
Ravi's distance = 8 × 3/5 = 4.8 km
Circular Track — Opposite Directions & Clock ReasoningCAT 2019 Slot 2Hard
Two ants A and B start from a point P on a circle at the same time, with A moving clock-wise and B moving anti-clockwise. They meet for the first time at 10:00 am when A has covered 60% of the track. If A returns to P at 10:12 am, then B returns to P at:
A) 10:27 am
B) 10:25 am
C) 10:45 am
D) 10:18 am
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: A) 10:27 am
At 10:00 AM: A covered 60%, B covered 40%. Speed ratio = 3:2 → Time ratio = 2:3.
A's remaining 40% takes 12 min → full lap = 12/0.4 = 30 min. So A started at 30−12 = 18 min before 10:00 = 9:42 AM.
B's full lap = 30 × 3/2 = 45 min. B returns to P = 9:42 + 45 = 10:27 AM

Ravi is driving at a speed of 40 km/h on a road. Vijay is 54 meters behind Ravi and driving in the same direction. Ashok is driving from the opposite direction at 50 km/h and is 225 meters from Ravi. The speed, in km/h, at which Vijay should drive so that all three cross each other at the same time is:
A) 58.8
B) 67.2
C) 61.6
D) 64.4
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: C) 61.6 km/h
Ravi-Ashok relative speed = 40+50 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Time to meet = 225/25 = 9 seconds.
Ravi travels 40 × 5/18 × 9 = 100 m in 9s. Meeting point is 100m ahead of Ravi.

Boats & Streams — Speed DoublingCAT 2017 Slot 1Hard
A man travels by a motor boat down a river to his office and back. With the speed of the river unchanged, if he doubles the speed of his motor boat, then his total travel time gets reduced by 75%. The ratio of the original speed of the motor boat to the speed of the river is:
A) √6 : √2
B) √7 : 2
C) 2√5 : 3
D) 3 : 2
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: B) √7 : 2
T₁ = 2Db/(b²−s²). T₂ = 4Db/(4b²−s²). Given T₂ = T₁/4:
4/(4b²−s²) = 1/[2(b²−s²)] → 8(b²−s²) = 4b²−s² → 4b² = 7s²
b/s = √7/2

Alligation on Two PurchasesCAT 2020 Slot 1Medium
A person spent Rs 50000 to purchase a desktop computer and a laptop computer. He sold the desktop at 20% profit and the laptop at 10% loss. If overall he made a 2% profit, then the purchase price, in rupees, of the desktop is: [TITA]
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 20,000
D + L = 50000. 0.20D − 0.10L = 1000 → 2D − L = 10000.
Adding: 3D = 60000 → D = 20,000.
Alligation shortcut: (+20%) and (−10%) give (+2%). Ratio = (2−(−10)):(20−2) = 12:18 = 2:3. Desktop = 2/5 × 50000 = 20000.
Markup, Discount, Wastage — Complex ScenarioCAT 2020 Slot 3Hard
A man buys 35 kg of sugar and sets a marked price in order to make a 20% profit. He sells 5 kg at this price, and 15 kg at a 10% discount. Accidentally, 3 kg of sugar is wasted. He sells the remaining sugar by raising the marked price by p percent so as to make an overall profit of 15%. Then p is nearest to:
A) 35
B) 31
C) 22
D) 25
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: D) 25
Let CP = ₹10/kg. Total CP = 350. Target 15% profit → Total SP = 402.50. MP = ₹12/kg.
Revenue: 5kg × 12 = 60. 15kg × 10.8 = 162. 3kg wasted = 0. Remaining = 12 kg.
Need from 12kg: 402.50 − 222 = 180.50 → SP/kg = 15.04.
Increase over MP of 12: (15.04−12)/12 × 100 ≈ 25%

Amal buys 110 kg of syrup and 120 kg of juice, syrup being 20% less costly per kg than juice. He sells 10 kg of syrup at 10% profit and 20 kg of juice at 20% profit. Mixing the remaining juice and syrup, he sells the mixture at ₹308.32 per kg and makes an overall profit of 64%. The cost price, in ₹, of a kg of syrup is: [TITA]

Anu, Vinu and Manu can complete a work alone in 15 days, 12 days and 20 days, respectively. Vinu works everyday. Anu works only on alternate days starting from the first day, while Manu works only on alternate days starting from the second day. Then, the number of days needed to complete the work is:
A) 5
B) 8
C) 6
D) 7
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: D) 7 days
Total work = LCM(15,12,20) = 60 units. Anu=4/day, Vinu=5/day, Manu=3/day.
Day 1 (Anu+Vinu): 9 | Day 2 (Manu+Vinu): 8 | 2-day cycle = 17 units.
3 cycles (6 days) = 51 units. Remaining = 9. Day 7: Anu+Vinu = 9 ✓ → 7 days
Relative Efficiency — John, Jack, JimCAT 2020 Slot 2Hard
John takes twice as much time as Jack to finish a job. Jack and Jim together take one-thirds of the time to finish the job than John takes working alone. Moreover, in order to finish the job, John takes three days more than that taken by three of them working together. In how many days will Jim finish the job working alone? [TITA]
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 4 days
R_Jack = 2·R_John. Jack+Jim rate = 3·R_John → R_Jim = R_John.
All three: 4·R_John → T_all = T_John/4.
T_John = T_John/4 + 3 → ¾·T_John = 3 → T_John = 4.
Since R_Jim = R_John → Jim takes 4 days
Staggered Joining + 10% Work ShareCAT 2019 Slot 2Medium
Anil alone can do a job in 20 days while Sunil alone can do it in 40 days. Anil starts the job, and after 3 days, Sunil joins him. Again, after a few more days, Bimal joins them and they together finish the job. If Bimal has done 10% of the job, then in how many days was the job done?
A) 13
B) 12
C) 15
D) 14
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: A) 13 days
Work = 40 units. Anil = 2/day, Sunil = 1/day. Bimal did 10% = 4 units.
Anil alone for 3 days = 6. Remaining for Anil+Sunil = 40−4−6 = 30 units at 3/day = 10 days.
Total = 3 + 10 = 13 days
Pipes & Cisterns — Complex SystemCAT 2023 Slot 2Hard
Pipes A and C are fill pipes while Pipe B is a drain pipe. Time taken by B to empty the full reservoir is 1 hour less than the time taken by A to fill it. When all three are turned on, the reservoir fills in 2 hours. If only B and C are turned on, and B is turned off after 1 hour, then C takes another 1 hour 15 minutes to fill it. The time taken, in minutes, by Pipe C alone to fill the reservoir is: [TITA]
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 90 minutes
All three: a − b + c = 1/2. B+C for 1hr then C for 5/4 hr: (c−b) + 5c/4 = 1 → 9c/4 − b = 1.
B empties 1 hr less than A fills: 1/b = 1/a − 1.
Solving the system: c = 2/3 → C alone = 1/(2/3) = 3/2 hr = 90 minutes
Man-Days — Additional WorkersCAT 2020 Slot 3Medium

Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 40
140 × 60 days → 1.5 km. Remaining: 4.5 km in 140 days.
M₂ × 140 / 4.5 = 140 × 60 / 1.5 → M₂ = 180. Additional = 180 − 140 = 40

How many three-digit numbers are greater than 100 and increase by 198 when the three digits are arranged in the reverse order? [TITA]

Answer: 70

Total = 7 × 10 = 70

Power Equivalence — N^N = 2^160CAT 2018 Slot 2Hard
If N and x are positive integers such that N^N = 2^160 and N² + 2^N is an integral multiple of 2^x, then the largest possible x is: [TITA]

Diophantine — Integer Solutions with ConstraintsCAT 2020 Slot 2Hard
The number of pairs of integers (x, y) satisfying x ≥ y ≥ −20 and 2x + 5y = 99 is: [TITA]
Show Answer & Solution
Answer: 17
Base solution: x=2, y=19. General: x = 2+5k, y = 19−2k.
x ≥ y → 2+5k ≥ 19−2k → k ≥ 3. y ≥ −20 → 19−2k ≥ −20 → k ≤ 19.
k ∈ [3, 19] → 17 pairs

Answer: 4
g(x) = 4x + 2a. g(20) = 80+2a = 72 → a = −4.

Simultaneous Logarithmic EquationsCAT 2019 Slot 1Hard

Set 1: Prime Token Funding 🔢

CAT 2022 Slot 1 • Type: Number Theory + Matrix • Difficulty: Hard
Setup: Six interviewers (Adhara, Bithi, Chhaya, Dhanavi, Esther, Fathima) each award tokens of one distinct prime value from {2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13}. Five candidates receive funding = 1000 × (product of token values received).

Pragnyaa: ₹390,000 (390=2×3×5×13) | Qahira: ₹210,000 (210=2×3×5×7) | Rasheeda: ₹165,000 (165=3×5×11) | Smera: ₹77,000 (77=7×11) | Tantra: ₹66,000 (66=2×3×11)

Key Constraints: Fathima awarded to everyone except Qahira. Adhara awarded only to Pragnyaa. Bithi awarded to Smera but not Qahira. You deduce which interviewer has which prime.

Set 2: Archery Tournament 🏹

CAT 2019 Slot 1 • Type: Tournament + Scoring • Difficulty: Hard
Setup: Six archers (Tanzi, Umeza, Wangdu, Xyla, Yonita, Zeneca) compete across Rounds 1-3 (mandatory) with scores 1-5. Each Bull's Eye (5) earns a bonus round (Rounds 4-6).

Constraints: Tanzi, Umeza, Yonita had identical final scores. All scores except one were multiples of 3. Highest total = 2×(lowest) + 1. Bull's eyes in Round 2 = 2× those in Round 3. Tanzi and Zeneca had same Round 1 scores but different Round 3 scores.

Set 3: Three Doctors Clinic Queue 🏥

CAT 2019 Slot 2 • Type: Scheduling / Queuing • Difficulty: Very Hard
Setup: Three doctors (Ben: 10min/₹100, Kane: 15min/₹200, Wayne: 25min/₹300) in 3 rooms, 9:00-11:30 AM. Patients get sequential tokens. When a room is free, next token enters immediately. If multiple rooms free simultaneously, lower-numbered room gets priority.

Questions asked: Which doctor did token #15 see? Total revenue by each doctor? Wait time for specific tokens?

Set 4: Police Station Patrolling Routes 🚔

CAT 2023 Slot 3 • Type: Routes & Networks • Difficulty: Hard
Setup: Six stations (A-F) connected by streets. Four teams patrol 09:00–12:00 hrs. Each street segment takes 30 min. All teams start and end at Station A (6 segments each). No two teams can be on the same segment simultaneously.

Constraints: Team locations given at key intervals. A-E segment used only by Teams 1 and 4. Team 4 never visited B, D, or F. Plot conflict-free routes for all four teams.

Set 5: ATM Cash Dispensation 💵

CAT 2018 Slot 1 • Type: System Combinatorics • Difficulty: Medium
Setup: ATM dispenses exactly ₹5,000 using only ₹100, ₹200, and ₹500 notes. Customer selects preferred denomination. Machine must give more notes of the preferred denomination than the other two combined.

Questions: How many valid combinations for ₹500 preference? Max customers with 50 ₹500 notes? Minimum total notes for ₹100 preference?

Set 6: Walkways & Lakes Community 🏘️

CAT 2024 Slot 2 • Type: Distance Optimization • Difficulty: Medium
Setup: Gated township with walkways and 3 rectangular lakes. Junctions A-P with given distances (OP=150m, ON=MN=300m, ML=400m, etc.). Gate at C, houses at A/H/M/L, Bank at B, Post Office at P.

Questions: Shortest round-trip from L visiting both P and B. Maximum non-repeating path along lake perimeters.

Set 7: Coloured Beads 5×5 Grid 🔴🔵🟢

CAT 2020 Slot 2 • Type: Constraint Satisfaction • Difficulty: Very Hard
Setup: 25 beads (Red, Blue, Green) in a 5×5 grid. Rules: (1) Adjacent cells can't have same colour. (2) Between any two Blues in a row/column, there must be at least one Green. (3) Between any two Reds, there must be at least one Blue AND one Green.

Questions: Max Red beads in entire grid? Min Blue beads required? How many valid 2-colour configurations?
